# Pinball Punch Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 12, 2026

## 1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Pinball Punch is developed and operated by Alexandre Bobeda, using the studio name AB Studio, in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. Alexandre Bobeda is the controller of personal data processed for the purposes described in this policy.

This policy covers the Pinball Punch Android game, its pages and website analytics on [alexandrebobeda.com](https://www.alexandrebobeda.com/), and communications about the game. It does not cover the operation of unrelated projects, other games, or external services linked from the developer's website.

For support, privacy questions, or requests concerning your personal data, email [pinball.punch@alexandrebobeda.com](mailto:pinball.punch@alexandrebobeda.com).

## 2. Game progress stored on your device

Pinball Punch stores game progress locally on your device, including scores, earned stars, unlocked levels, completed-game records, and records used to grant gameplay rewards correctly. This information lets you continue playing and retain your achievements.

We do not upload local save files to an AB Studio server. The launch version does not offer player accounts, cloud saves, online leaderboards, in-app purchases, or subscriptions. It does not include a separate crash-reporting service. If you choose to enable optional game analytics, selected score and gameplay information is sent to Google as described in section 5; this does not create a cloud backup. Advertising services can also collect the technical and interaction data described below.

You can remove locally stored progress by clearing Pinball Punch's app storage through Android settings or uninstalling the game. Doing so deletes your local progress, and we cannot restore it from our servers.

## 3. Optional rewarded advertising

Advertising is Pinball Punch's launch revenue source. The game uses Google AdMob, through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, to provide optional rewarded advertisements. You can choose to watch an advertisement for a gameplay benefit, such as a Ball Save or Rewarded Ball. Watching advertisements is not required to play, unlock levels, or earn stars.

Google's advertising services may collect or receive:

- Your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location.
- Device and advertising identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID, subject to device settings, consent, and SDK configuration. Google may also receive identifiers associated with signed-in accounts on the device where applicable; this does not create an AB Studio player account.
- App and advertisement interactions, such as launches, taps, video views, and ad completion events.
- Diagnostic and performance information, such as startup time, responsiveness, and energy usage.

Google uses this information to deliver and measure advertisements, operate and improve its advertising services, detect fraud, and protect those services. Advertising identifiers may support personalized advertising when permitted. App set IDs are used only for permitted purposes such as analytics and fraud prevention, not advertising personalization.

AdMob can process data when the advertising SDK is initialized or an advertisement is requested, including when an ad is loaded before you decide to watch it. Choosing not to watch an advertisement does not by itself stop all advertising SDK processing. Required consent and age restrictions apply before the relevant processing begins.

We use AdMob without integrating additional third-party advertising networks at launch. Google may work with advertisers and advertising technology providers as part of delivering advertisements. Applicable providers and choices are disclosed through the advertising consent interface where required.

Learn more in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [Google's advertising information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads), and [Google Mobile Ads data disclosures](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure).

## 4. Personalized ads, consent, and your choices

Personalized advertisements may be offered to eligible adults aged 18 or older where permitted by law and Google policy. Where consent is required, we obtain it before using personal data or device storage for those purposes. We do not use behavioral profiling to target commercial advertisements to players under 18. If adult eligibility has not been established, personalized advertising is not enabled.

The game and its consent tools may process and store an age-eligibility category, your privacy choices, and records of consent to apply the correct advertising restrictions and remember your choices. Age-related signals may be sent to Google to restrict advertising. We use this information to apply privacy protections, not to create an advertising profile of a younger player.

The game provides an Ad Privacy menu for reviewing your age category, personalization preference, optional game analytics choice, and, where applicable, the regional advertising choices. You can use it to review your choices, refuse optional processing, or withdraw consent. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before you withdrew consent. Accepting the Terms of Service or choosing a gameplay reward does not constitute consent to personalized advertising or optional game analytics.

Depending on your choices and applicable requirements, the game may offer non-personalized ads, limited ads, or no ads. Non-personalized ads can still involve device storage and data processing; they are not automatically exempt from consent requirements. Ad-funded rewards may be unavailable if no permitted advertisement can be served, but ordinary gameplay remains available.

You can also manage or delete your advertising ID through Android settings and review Google's [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/). These controls supplement the game's privacy choices; they do not necessarily stop all advertising data processing.

## 5. Optional game analytics and feature configuration

Pinball Punch uses Google Analytics for Firebase to measure how the game and rewarded offers are used. Game analytics is disabled by default, including automatic Analytics collection before your choice. It is enabled only after a player has confirmed they are aged 18 or older and separately allowed game analytics. It remains disabled for players under 18 and when adult eligibility is unknown. The game offers this optional choice after adult age confirmation, and eligible adults can change it through GAME ANALYTICS in the Ad Privacy menu.

When enabled, Analytics may process:

- An app-instance identifier and device and app information, such as the device model, operating system, app version, and general region.
- App usage and timing information, such as app opens, sessions, activity, and return visits.
- Gameplay information, such as level, score, score changes, reward use, and the reward-offer variant applied to a run.
- Rewarded-ad interactions, such as an offer being shown, a player choosing it, completion, errors, impressions, and ad revenue reported by the advertising service.

We use these measurements to compare reward offers, understand player progression and return visits, and assess advertising performance. Custom gameplay events do not include your date of birth, a player-account identifier, or the game's individual run identifiers. This analytics choice is separate from personalized-ad consent: allowing one does not allow the other. Refusing or withdrawing game analytics consent does not prevent ordinary gameplay or, where otherwise permitted, rewarded advertisements.

Disabling GAME ANALYTICS stops future Analytics collection, clears Analytics data stored by the SDK on your device, and resets its app-instance identifier. This does not delete game progress or automatically erase data previously received by Google. Provider-held data and deletion requests are addressed in section 10. See [Google Analytics for Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/docs/analytics) and [Firebase Analytics collection controls](https://firebase.google.com/docs/analytics/android/configure-data-collection).

The game also uses Firebase Remote Config to receive feature settings, including whether a run can offer one rewarded benefit or both Ball Save and Rewarded Ball. A run keeps the rules selected at its start. Configuration delivery is separate from optional game analytics and can operate when game analytics is declined. Google may process a Firebase installation identifier and technical connection information to provide this service. The configuration contains feature settings, not sensitive player information or local save files. See [Firebase's privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) and [Firebase Remote Config](https://firebase.google.com/docs/remote-config).

## 6. Pinball Punch website pages and cookies

The developer's website is hosted on Vercel and uses Google Analytics. When you visit Pinball Punch pages on that website, hosting and analytics services can process technical and usage information, including requested pages, timestamps, referrers, browser and device information, general location, and network information needed to deliver and secure the pages.

Google Analytics is the website's analytics service. It uses the following first-party analytics cookies to measure visits and interactions and produce website usage reports:

- **_ga:** Distinguishes visitors. Configured expiration: 2 years.
- **_ga_0DB7K5WVMY:** Maintains session status and analytics state for the website's Google Analytics data stream. Configured expiration: 2 years.

Browser limits and your deletion choices can shorten these lifetimes, and cookies may be renewed on subsequent visits. Cookie expiration is separate from the retention of analytics data on Google's servers. See [Google Analytics cookie information](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11397207).

Vercel provides website hosting and security services. Vercel Web Analytics is not used on these pages. Hosting and security processing is separate from Google Analytics measurement.

You can manage, block, or delete analytics cookies through your browser's privacy controls and use the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). Blocking cookies alone does not necessarily prevent every analytics request. These controls do not stop hosting and security processing needed to provide the website. For questions or requests about website analytics data under our control, contact [pinball.punch@alexandrebobeda.com](mailto:pinball.punch@alexandrebobeda.com).

Website cookie choices, optional game analytics consent, and game advertising choices are separate. Visiting these pages or reading this policy does not give consent to optional game analytics or personalized advertisements in the game.

For provider information, see [Google Analytics privacy information](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/6004245?hl=en) and [Vercel's Privacy Policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy).

These grounds do not override requirements to obtain consent or protections that apply to minors.

## 10. Retention and deletion

Local game progress remains on your device until you clear app storage, uninstall the game, or otherwise remove that data. We cannot remotely delete records held only on your device.

We retain support correspondence only for as long as needed to resolve the request and for any further period necessary to meet legal obligations or handle related disputes. The period depends on the request, whether follow-up is needed, and applicable legal requirements. Information no longer needed for those purposes is deleted.

Privacy preferences, consent records, and age-eligibility information are kept for as long as needed to remember valid choices, apply advertising and analytics restrictions, and meet applicable recordkeeping requirements. Records stored only in app storage are removed when you clear that storage or uninstall; provider-held records may remain subject to their own retention rules.

For both the game and website Google Analytics properties, the configured retention periods are **2 months for event data** and **14 months for user data**. Deletion and any renewal of user-data retention follow the service's retention settings and processing schedule. Standard aggregated reports can have different retention from user-level or event-level records. These server-side periods are separate from the website analytics cookies' two-year configured expiry.

Advertising records held by Google are subject to Google's retention rules and the applicable advertising settings. Firebase installation identifiers used for Remote Config have separate provider retention and deletion rules; disabling game analytics does not delete that installation identifier. Clearing app storage, uninstalling the game, or resetting local Analytics data does not automatically delete data already held by these providers. Provider retention can vary by data type and purpose; see [Google's retention information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention), [Google Analytics retention settings](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/7667196?hl=en), and the provider information linked above.

To request deletion of personal data under our control, email [pinball.punch@alexandrebobeda.com](mailto:pinball.punch@alexandrebobeda.com). We will address the request under applicable law, explain any lawful retention exceptions, and assist with relevant provider requests where appropriate. Information irreversibly anonymized so that it no longer relates to an identifiable person may be retained as aggregate statistics.

## 13. Younger players

Pinball Punch is intended for teenagers and adults aged 13 or older and is not directed to children under 13. If your local law requires a higher age or a parent or guardian's involvement for particular processing or use of the service, those requirements apply.
